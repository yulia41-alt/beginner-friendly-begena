Learn Begena

The Begena is an ancient, ten-stringed Ethiopian lyre traditionally played for spirtiual contemplation, meditation and prayer. Known as King David's Harp, it produce a distinct, deep-pitched buzzing drone that creates a deeply reflective and sacred soundscape.

Start easy

Begena normally has 3 styles
1. Selamta: this is a welcoming and structural style.
2. Wane: it has deeply emotional and mournful style.
3. Medina: it rhythmic and highly narrative style.
